After the events of Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins, Mario was gifted a pet dog named 'Lucky'. Mario loved little Lucky more than anything. But one day, a strange flying ship approached Mario's castle. It belonged to Tantanga, but something didn't seem right. The ship crashed through the castle wall and kidnapped Lucky. All Mario heard was a muffled "WA HA HA- (cough) ha- (double cough) ha..." Wario?! That evil fellow was heading towards Lost Land, the unknown territories of Sarasa Land — a place even Bowser himself wouldn't dare to go alone. Mario had no idea how to find Lucky now, as there was no official map of it. But he noticed one thing: fish heads? Lucky must have left them behind! Mario began his journey into the depths of Lost Land. Will you help him get through this weird Adventure safely?
